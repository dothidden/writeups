# .hidden CTF Team

[.hidden](/about) was initially formed on the 28th of April 2023, when a group of students from the University of Bucharest randomly decided to participate in a CTF competition. We have participated in over 50 CTF's and reached the thrid place in the country rankings on [CTFtime](https://ctftime.org/team/225182).

However, over the years, we have participated in less and less competitions, and our focus has shifted towards learning and sharing knowledge with the community.
